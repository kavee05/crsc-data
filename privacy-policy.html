CRSC Privacy Policy

CRSC: Car Related Sales Calculator · Effective 19 October 2026

In short: CRSC has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or photos. The cars and calculations you save stay on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sends anonymous crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics.

What stays on your phone

Saved cars, saved calculations and your settings (theme, region, fuel price preference) are stored only on your device. They can be included in your own Android device backup. You can delete them at any time with Settings → Clear my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Public data the app downloads

To show up-to-date information, the app downloads public data. It sends no personal information to these sources:

  • Fuel prices from the Malaysian government open data portal (data.gov.my).
  • The Overnight Policy Rate from the Bank Negara Malaysia Open API.
  • Car prices, road tax rates and typical loan rates from the CRSC data site on GitHub Pages. The prices are collected weekly from the car brands' official websites.

Third-party services

Google AdMob shows banner ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), and how you interact with ads, in order to serve and measure ads. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent first through Google's consent form. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and basic device information (such as model and Android version) when the app crashes, so bugs can be fixed. Crash reports are not linked to your identity. See the Firebase privacy information.

Data security

All network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

CRSC is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Accuracy

Calculations are estimates. Road tax at renewal is set by JPJ (MyJPJ), loan terms by your bank, and insurance premiums by your insurer.
